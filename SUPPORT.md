# Getting help with StoryStage 2

## Teachers, parents, schools and clubs

- **Getting started:** [storystage.org](https://storystage.org), for the set-up steps and the teacher guide.
- **A question, a problem or an idea:** email **feedback@storystage.org**. One email per question helps us keep track. We read everything; we can't promise to answer every message, but we answer questions as soon as we can, usually within a week.
- **Something not working?** Say what you were doing, what happened, and the device (for example "a Chromebook" or "an iPad"). In StoryStage, **Settings › Report a problem** makes a report about the device (versions, settings and a self-test) to copy into your email or attach. It leaves out the Claude key and pupils' names; read it before you send it.
- **Please don't send** children's names, photos, voices or school details. We don't need them to help.

## Learners

Ask your teacher, or a parent or carer. They can email us for you.

## Things we can't do

- **Get back lost work.** StoryStage has no server or accounts, so a production lives only on the device or wherever it was saved. Collecting a class's work into the school's own storage regularly, as the teacher guide describes, keeps a copy.
- **Help with a Claude account or bill.** The Prompter uses your school's or family's own Claude key, so questions about the key, usage or billing go to [Anthropic's support](https://support.anthropic.com).

## Developers

- **Found a bug, or have an idea?** Open an issue with one of the forms; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **A security problem:** report it privately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## A child's safety

If you're worried about a child's safety, follow "Children and safeguarding" in the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md): your school's safeguarding procedure first, then tell us privately.
