# 0039 Age range (confirmed)

- **Status:** settled; supersedes [0030](0030-age-range.md)
- **Decided:** 25 Oct 2026, by the owner
- **Source:** the owner's answer in session D, on the curriculum mapping (WP16a, `docs/curriculum/mapping.md` section 7, #68)

## Decision

StoryStage 2 is designed for learners aged **6 to 13**. The provisional range in 0030 is confirmed.

## Why

The curriculum mapping (WP16.1–16.5) found strong touch points in every UK nation from the start of formal schooling (England Year 1, Scotland First level, Wales progression step 1, Northern Ireland Key Stage 1) to age 14 (KS3, Third level, step 4). Learners aged 6–7 need teacher-led use and reading support (voice input and captions). The weaker KS3 computing fit is met by the pathway to text programming (16.8, WP20), not by widening the range.

The teacher interviews (16.7) can still reopen it, through a new record.
