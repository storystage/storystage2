# 0043 App store regions

- **Status:** settled
- **Decided:** 25 Oct 2026, by the owner
- **Source:** the owner's answer in session D, on Anthropic's terms for under-18s (`docs/legal/anthropic-terms-under-18s.md` section 4, #52)

## Decision

The first release's apps are offered in the **UK and US** app stores. (The recommendation had been the UK only.)

## What follows

- **Privacy:** the DPIA (WP17.3) gets a US section, reviewed by someone who knows US law: COPPA and its 2025 rule changes (who counts as the "operator" when the Prompter sends a child's text to Anthropic, and what parental consent the adult set-up gives), and US state children's privacy laws (requirement R10 in the Anthropic note).
- **Store builds** (S8, WP15.4–15.6): Apple's Kids category rules and Google Play's Families policy (no third-party analytics or ads, a parental gate before links leave the app, accurate privacy labels).
- **Trade mark** (WP17.6): a US search and US filing advice, because a US application for STORY STAGE (class 28) exists (`docs/legal/trade-mark-storystage.md`).
- **Other countries** need a fresh check before they are added.
