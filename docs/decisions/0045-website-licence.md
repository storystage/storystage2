# 0045 Licence for the website and teacher guide

- **Status:** settled
- **Decided:** 25 Oct 2026, by the owner
- **Source:** the owner's answer in session D, on the WP19a drafts (#79, #81)

## Decision

The text of the website and the teacher guide (`website/`) is published under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**, like the curriculum material (0041). Schools may copy and adapt it, crediting "From StoryStage (storystage.org), CC BY 4.0". The StoryStage name and logo are not covered by the licence, and the trade mark question (WP17.6) is separate. The code stays under Apache-2.0 (0025).
