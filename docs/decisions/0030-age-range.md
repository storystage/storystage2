## Decision

StoryStage 2 is designed for learners aged **6 to 13**, as the Direction A handoff assumes. This is provisional: test it against the full curriculum review across the four UK nations (WP16a, 16.1–16.5) and the teacher interviews (16.7), then confirm or change it in a new record.

Until then, design, wording and the curriculum mapping work to 6–13.
