## Decision

The library of 3D models is called **Backstage**, in four sections: **Cast** (anything that can act), **Props** (things characters pick up, use or ride), **Set** (scenery: buildings, trees, furniture fixed in place) and **Backdrops** (ground, sky, lighting and fog). Each model has one home section; search covers all four. The sketch library, the Prompter's catalogue and film credits use the same sections.
