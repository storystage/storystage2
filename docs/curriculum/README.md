# Curriculum mapping: the four UK nations (WP16)

Session D keeps this folder. It shows where StoryStage 2 meets each nation's curriculum, so teachers can see why it belongs in their lessons, and so the curriculum arc templates (16.6) and the storybook cast (WP3.6) are chosen from real needs.

- **Workplan:** WP16a, tasks 16.1–16.5 (this folder); WP16b, task 16.6 (templates and use cases) follows.
- **Decisions:** 0018 (map all four nations; the 3- and 5-part arc templates ship regardless; curriculum templates follow the mapping); 0030 (learners aged 6 to 13, provisional until this review and the teacher interviews, 16.7).
- **Done when (WP16):** the mapping is published as a doc, cited to the official curriculum documents, and the owner has chosen the curriculum templates to ship.

## Method

1. **The curriculum in force.** Each page maps the curriculum schools teach now, which is the one in force for the pilot (spring 2027) and the first release's first school year. Each page also says what reform is under way and when it lands, so the mapping can be redone (see "Reform watch" below).
2. **Cite, don't copy.** Every row names the official document, the key stage or level, and the section, with a link, and gives the statement **in a short paraphrase**. Where the nation numbers its statements (Scotland's experiences and outcomes, for example `TCH 1-14a`), the row gives that code. Teachers should read the official wording; the paraphrase is only there to make the table usable.
3. **Our own ids.** Each row has an id: nation, subject, stage, number (for example `EN-CO-KS2-3`). The ids are ours, for cross-reference from `mapping.md`, the templates and the teacher guide; they aren't official.
4. **How well it fits.**
   - **Strong:** a StoryStage activity meets the statement's main aim as it stands.
   - **Partial:** it meets part of the statement, or only with the teacher's framing.

   Statements with no touch point are listed at the end of each page, so nobody over-claims.
5. **Ages.** Rows give the age range of the stage, cut to StoryStage's 6–13. A row outside 6–13 is kept only if it changes the age decision (0030).
6. **Features as planned.** The mapping uses the first release's features as the workplan describes them ([features.md](features.md)). Features planned for after the release candidate (AR, projection, the pathway exports) are marked **later**, so teachers aren't promised them for the pilot.

## How the stages line up with ages 6 to 13

| Age (start of the school year) | England | Scotland (CfE level) | Wales (progression step, roughly) | Northern Ireland |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5–6 | Year 1 (KS1) | P2 (First) | Step 1 to 2 | P2 (Foundation Stage) |
| 6–7 | Year 2 (KS1) | P3 (First) | Step 2 | P3 (KS1) |
| 7–8 | Year 3 (KS2) | P4 (First) | Step 2 to 3 | P4 (KS1) |
| 8–9 | Year 4 (KS2) | P5 (Second) | Step 3 | P5 (KS2) |
| 9–10 | Year 5 (KS2) | P6 (Second) | Step 3 | P6 (KS2) |
| 10–11 | Year 6 (KS2) | P7 (Second) | Step 3 to 4 | P7 (KS2) |
| 11–12 | Year 7 (KS3) | S1 (Third) | Step 4 | Year 8 (KS3) |
| 12–13 | Year 8 (KS3) | S2 (Third) | Step 4 | Year 9 (KS3) |
| 13–14 | Year 9 (KS3) | S3 (Third and Fourth) | Step 4 to 5 | Year 10 (KS3) |

Learners aged 6 to 13 are in school years from England's Year 1 or 2 to Year 8 or 9. Scotland's levels are broad and a learner may work at a different level from their stage; Wales's progression steps are reference points at roughly ages 5, 8, 11, 14 and 16, not year groups.

## Reform watch (checked 25 October 2026)

| Nation | In force now | What's coming | Effect on this mapping |
|---|---|---|---|
| England | National curriculum 2014 (computing and English programmes from September 2013) | After the Curriculum and Assessment Review (final report, November 2025), the DfE plans draft programmes for consultation "later this year" (Tes, October 2026), final programmes in 2027, and first teaching from September 2028 | Remap when the drafts are published; the pilot and the first release's first year use the 2014 programmes |
| Scotland | Curriculum for Excellence experiences and outcomes, and benchmarks | The Curriculum Improvement Cycle: a "Know, Do, Understand" framework with Big Ideas, drafts shared from June 2026, co-created until January 2027; experiences and outcomes phased out from August 2028 | Remap against the framework once it is final; until then, the codes here are the ones teachers use |
| Wales | Curriculum for Wales (from 2022, all years by 2026) and the Digital Competence Framework | No replacement planned | Stable |
| Northern Ireland | The Northern Ireland Curriculum (2007) | A draft Northern Ireland Curriculum Framework 2028, consulted on until 30 September 2026, from the Curriculum Taskforce | Remap when the final framework is published |
