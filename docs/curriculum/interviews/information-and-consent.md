## StoryStage teacher interviews: information for you

**What is this?** StoryStage is a free tool for learners aged 6 to 13 to make 3D animated films with code, being developed for schools across the UK. Before it goes into schools, we want to understand how teachers actually teach storytelling and coding, and what gets in their way. We're asking five teachers and club leaders to talk to us.

**What would I do?** Talk with **[name]** for about 45 minutes, by video call or in person, about your teaching, then look at some pictures of StoryStage and tell us what you think. There is nothing to prepare.

**Do I have to?** No. You can stop at any time, skip any question, and withdraw what you've said up to **[date: two weeks after the interview]**, without giving a reason.

**What happens to what I say?**

- We write notes, and with your permission an audio recording, which is used only to check the notes and is **deleted within 30 days**.
- The write-up uses a letter ("Teacher A"), your role, the age group you teach and your nation. It doesn't include your name, your school, your town, or any pupil.
- The write-up is published in StoryStage's public project documents, and may shape the design, the lesson plans and the website. We may quote you, without your name.
- We keep your name and contact details privately, only to contact you about this, and delete them when the work ends (by **[date]**) or when you ask.
- Your notes are never pasted into an AI tool with your name in them.

**Please don't** tell us pupils' names or anything that would identify a pupil.

**Who is responsible?** **[Owner's name], trading as StoryStage**, contact **[email address]**. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
