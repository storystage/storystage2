# Scotland: Curriculum for Excellence (WP16.2)

- **Workplan:** 16.2, "Curriculum for Excellence experiences and outcomes and benchmarks, for technologies (computing science), literacy and English, and expressive arts (drama)".
- **Checked:** 25 October 2026, against Education Scotland's documents (below). Method, fit levels and ids: [README.md](README.md). Features, activities and arc templates: [features.md](features.md).
- **In force:** the experiences and outcomes and the benchmarks. The Curriculum Improvement Cycle is replacing them with a "Know, Do, Understand" framework, phased in from August 2028 (README, "Reform watch").
- **Codes:** each row gives the official code of the experience and outcome (for example `TCH 1-14a`: technologies, First level, organiser 14, statement a). Where a benchmark adds detail that matters for StoryStage, the row says so.

## Levels and ages

| Level | Stages | Ages (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Pre-school to P1 | 3–5 |
| First | P2 to P4 | 5–8 |
| Second | P5 to P7 | 8–11 |
| Third (and Fourth) | S1 to S3 | 11–14 |

StoryStage's 6–13 covers First, Second and Third level. A learner may work at a level other than the usual one for their stage.

**Films are texts.** Literacy and English's principles and practice paper lists films, games and TV programmes among the texts learners read and create. A learner's StoryStage film is therefore a text in its own right, not just a way of presenting one, which is why the "creating texts" outcomes below fit strongly.

## No touch point

`TCH 1-14b`, `2-14b`, `3-14b` (how computers process and store information, networks, computer architecture); the web mark-up and list parts of `TCH 3-14a` and `3-15a`; Fourth level throughout (beyond 6–13); the music and dance outcomes of expressive arts; the tools-for-writing outcomes on spelling and punctuation (`LIT x-21a`, `x-22a`), which StoryStage neither teaches nor checks.

## What Scotland shows

- **Computing science is the strongest fit of any nation.** The First and Second level benchmarks for `TCH 1-14a`, `2-14a` and `2-15a` read almost like a description of StoryStage: predict the movement and visual effect of a visual program, explain how parallel activities interact (do together), and find the mismatch between the task and the program (the storyboard check, `F-CHK`).
- **Literacy fits strongly because a film is a text.** `ENG x-31a` (stories, poems and plays with structure, characters and settings) and `LIT x-20a` (creating texts of their choice) run from First to Third level.
- **Drama fits well** through the script, rehearsal and screening (`EXA x-14a`, `x-15a`, `x-01a`); playing a role in person (`EXA x-12a`) only partly.
- **History and science at First and Second level** have two of the clearest cross-curricular uses: recreating a historical setting (`SOC 1-04a`) and modelling the solar system (`SCN 2-06a`).
- **For the age decision (0030):** the fit is strong from First level (P2, age 5–6) to Third level (S1–S3), so 6–13 holds.
