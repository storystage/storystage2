**Subject:** Permission to include Quaternius models in StoryStage 2, a free tool for schools

Hello,

I lead StoryStage, a free, open-source tool (Apache-2.0) that helps young people aged 6 to 13 in UK schools and clubs make short 3D animated films. It has no accounts, adverts or data collection.

StoryStage 2 includes a built-in model library, **Backstage**, which learners choose characters and props from. We would love to include some of your animated characters. Section 3(a) of your licence rules out redistributing the assets as an asset pack, so we are asking before we do anything. Would you allow us to:

1. include selected Quaternius models in Backstage, converted to glTF and simplified to run on low-cost school devices, with your name shown on each model;
2. let learners use them in the films they make, and share those films (video files and simple AR scenes, which contain the models) outside school;
3. let learners export their scenes to engines such as Unity, Unreal or Godot for further study, with your credit kept?

The models would leave StoryStage only inside a learner's own film or scene, never as a separate download.

We will credit you as "<Model> by Quaternius", or however you prefer. Thank you for considering this.
