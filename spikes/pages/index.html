StoryStage 2 spikes

Throwaway test pages for the device runs in docs/test/M1-spikes.md. They collect nothing and send nothing anywhere; each shows its results on the page.

  • 1.2 Characters: walking figures in four sizes, for the budget sweep (about 30 seconds).
  • 1.3 Speech to text: whisper on the device, with test clips and your own voice (about 110 MB the first time; it reloads itself once).
  • 1.4 Interpreter and scheduler: timing and repeatable frames (under a minute).
  • 1.5 Tile editor (option C) and Blockly (option B): try them by touch.
  • 1.8 MP4 export: makes a 10-second video you can download and play (a few minutes).
  • 14.3 Voices: Kokoro speaks ten lines in British voices, and the child and animal presets, each with a player (about 90 MB the first time; a few minutes). Not part of the M1 runs, but useful on the Chromebook for its speed.
  • 23.1 Projection bake: bakes a box and a building front for projection mapping and shows each step as pictures (under a minute). Not part of the M1 runs.
