StoryStage 2 contains no Alice code. It uses the third-party software listed below.

How this file is made: the two JavaScript sections at the end are generated from
pnpm-lock.yaml by `pnpm notice`, and CI fails when they are out of date or when a licence
breaks the policy (CLAUDE.md rule 8; tools/repo/src/licences.ts). This header is
tools/repo/notice-header.txt. Rust crates in apps/desktop are checked
against the same policy by cargo-deny (apps/desktop/src-tauri/deny.toml) and are listed in
the "Native layer" section, kept by hand until the app bundles carry their own notices (WP15).
Backstage models and sketches carry their licence, credit line and source in backstage.json.

Each line: package  version(s)  licence.
