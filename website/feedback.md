# Feedback and problems

We'd love to hear how StoryStage works in your classroom, club or home: what goes wrong, what gets in a learner's way, and what you'd change.

**Email us at [feedback@storystage.org].** One email per problem or idea helps us keep track. We read everything; we can't promise to reply to every message, but we'll answer questions about privacy and safeguarding.

## What to send

| You want to tell us about | Please include |
|---|---|
| **Something that went wrong** | What you were doing, what happened, and what you expected. The device (for example "Chromebook", "iPad", "Windows laptop"), and whether it was the app or the browser version. **The Report a problem file,** if you can (below) |
| **Something that gets in a learner's way** (accessibility) | What the learner was trying to do, and what stopped them: for example the keyboard, reading, hearing, colours, or the size of things on screen. Which assistive technology, if any |
| **A lesson, or the curriculum** | Which use case or story template, the age group and nation, and what worked or what you'd change |
| **An idea** | What you'd like StoryStage to do, and why it would help your learners |
| **Something worrying the Prompter said** | What was asked and what it said, as closely as you can. Please also follow your school's safeguarding procedure |

## The Report a problem file

In StoryStage, go to **Settings › Report a problem**. It makes a file on the device with the versions, the settings in force and the result of a quick check of the app. It leaves out the Claude key and personal details, and it doesn't include the learner's film unless you add it. Nothing is sent automatically: attach the file to your email if you're happy to.

## Please don't send

- **Pupils' names,** or anything else that identifies a pupil.
- **Photos or videos of pupils,** or films in which pupils can be seen or heard.
- **A Claude key,** ever.

If a film helps explain a problem, send one with no names in the credits and no one in it, or describe it instead.

## What happens next

We copy the useful part of your email into our public list of issues on GitHub, **without your name, your school or anything that identifies anyone**, and use it to plan the next release. We keep your email only until the issue is closed, plus 12 months, then delete it. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

## Security problems

If you find a way for StoryStage to leak a Claude key or learners' data, please don't email it: report it privately through GitHub (the **Security** tab of [github.com/storystage/storystage2](https://github.com/storystage/storystage2), then **Report a vulnerability**).
