# Teacher guide

For teachers, club leaders and teaching assistants using StoryStage with learners aged 6 to 13. No experience of animation or coding is needed.

**[Draft, WP19.2. Written from the approved mock-ups (decision 0044); real screenshots replace them when the app exists. The pages for each step of setting up and running a class (WP24.7) are added once the teacher's journey (24.1) is written.]**

## Start here

1. **Try it yourself first.** Open the browser version, or the app, choose **I'm a learner**, and follow the first guided film in **Learn** (about 15 minutes).
2. **Choose a use case.** Eight ready-made lesson sequences, for ages 6 to 13, are in [The curriculum](08-curriculum.md).
3. **Set up the class.** Choose the default code editor and how much of the Prompter pupils get. [Class set-up](06-class-set-up.md)
4. **Teach storyboard first.** Every film starts on paper, or rather on the Storyboard, and the code waits until it's planned. [Storyboard first](02-storyboard-first.md)
5. **Screen the films together.** The Screening room is where learners check their work and give each other feedback. [The Screening room](04-screening-room.md)
