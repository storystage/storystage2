# Privacy at a glance

**[Open: this is a summary drafted before the data protection impact assessment and the full privacy notice (WP17.3, 17.4). It is not published until the owner approves both. The full notice replaces the "more detail" links below.]**

StoryStage is made for children aged 6 to 13, so it is built to collect as little as possible, following the ICO's Age appropriate design code (the Children's code).

## The short version

- **No accounts.** Nobody signs up or logs in to use StoryStage.
- **No StoryStage server.** Films, storyboards and code stay on the device, or wherever you save them. We never receive them.
- **No analytics, adverts or tracking,** in the apps or on this website. The website sets no cookies.
- **The Prompter is optional and off by default.** It works only once an adult has added their own Claude key.

## What stays on the device

- **Productions** (storyboards, scripts, code, and any models a teacher imported) are saved as `.storystage` files on the device or where you choose. **[Open: where pupils' work is kept between lessons on shared school devices (WP24.2).]**
- **Voice input** is turned into text on the device. The audio is thrown away straight after, and never leaves the device.
- **Study logs,** used only in the pilot study, stay on the device until a teacher exports them. They record Prompter use and checks under a made-up code, not pupils' names.

## What the Prompter sends, and to whom

When a learner asks the Prompter something, the app sends the request to **Anthropic**, the company that makes Claude, under the Claude account of the adult who added the key. It sends text only: the request, and the storyboard, script and code it needs to answer. **It never sends pictures, camera images or audio.**

- Anthropic handles this under the key holder's agreement with Anthropic. Anthropic's terms say it doesn't train its models on this content.
- StoryStage can't see these requests, and doesn't keep them anywhere but the device.
- Learners shouldn't type names, addresses or other personal details into the Prompter, and the Prompter is told never to ask for them.
- The Prompter always says it is an AI, not a person.

## The browser version

The browser version has no Prompter and no voice input, so nothing a learner does leaves the device. Like any website, the server that delivers it keeps ordinary logs (for example, IP addresses). The browser version and this website are hosted by Bluehost. **[Open: how long Bluehost keeps its logs, and where, in the full notice (17.4).]**

## Children, parents and teachers

- An adult sets up the Prompter and agrees to its use; a learner can't.
- Teachers choose how much of the Prompter each class gets, or switch it off; schools can lock it.
