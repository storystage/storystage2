## Inspired by Alice

StoryStage grew out of **Alice**, the 3D programming environment from Carnegie Mellon University ([alice.org](https://www.alice.org)). StoryStage 1 was a fork of Alice 3. StoryStage 2 is written from scratch and contains no Alice code, but it keeps Alice's ideas: the names of its actions, "do in order" and "do together", and the design of its tile editor.
